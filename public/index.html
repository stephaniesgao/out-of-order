    How it works

    Each card stands for a number. Arrange the five numbers from largest to smallest, then check your order.

    A card in the correct position turns green and stays put. Reorder the rest. You have three checks to get all five right.

    The units are part of each fact. Compare the stated numbers: for example, 10 hours goes before 24 satellites. Rounded facts say so in their titles or answer notes.

    Drag by the grip, use the up and down buttons, or focus a card and press the arrow keys. Locked cards stay in their slots.

    Everyone gets the same daily puzzle at 00:00 UTC. Practice and challenge links use the same small fact bank, so facts can repeat. Completed daily games join the comparison automatically. The daily can be played once per signed-in account, or per browser for guests. Each check is remembered by the server. Returning resumes your attempt or opens its completed result. Practice is not ranked. Percentiles split tied results evenly.

    About

    A small ranking game with 32 everyday facts and simple comparisons. Each set has three familiar starting points, one medium question, and one surprise. The numbers and explanations appear when the game ends.

    Practice result links let someone else play your exact puzzle. Daily results compare how many checks each player needed. A result with fewer checks ranks higher; all unsolved games tie. Your percentile compares your result with other daily finishers, excluding your own entry. Tied comparisons are split evenly. Percentiles appear once at least five other daily results are available. The check-distribution percentages include your game.

    No ads or analytics. Daily comparison records contain a pseudonymous player key, puzzle date, checks used, solved status, and which first-check placements were correct. Names, emails, and IP addresses are not stored by the game. A persistent HTTP-only cookie identifies guest browsers. Clearing it can make a guest count as a new player. Daily attempts are stored on the server so refreshing or clearing local progress does not restart them. Personal stats stay in this browser.
